Rent Bill / Payment Entry

The total rent due through the current month is calculated automatically from the agreement start date and monthly rent. You may also add a monthly rent charge; the same month will not be counted twice. When payment is received, select Advance Rent Received, Partial Rent Received, or Rent Received.
